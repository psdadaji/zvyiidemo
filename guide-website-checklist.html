The 9-point website checklist

4 min read · By the Zvyii team

Why this matters

Most visitors decide within seconds whether a website feels trustworthy and useful. These nine checks cover the things that most often cost businesses enquiries.

The checklist

  1. A clear headline. A visitor should understand what you do and who it is for in five seconds.
  2. Mobile first. Most visitors in India browse on phones. Test your site on one.
  3. Fast loading. Compress images and avoid heavy sliders.
  4. One-tap contact. A visible call button and a WhatsApp button.
  5. Trust signals. Real photos, address, reviews and any certifications.
  6. Clear service pages. One page per main service, with a clear next step.
  7. SEO basics. Unique page titles and descriptions, and a Google Business Profile.
  8. Analytics. Track visits, calls and form submissions.
  9. Security. HTTPS enabled and software kept updated.

How to use it

Give your site a point for each item. Seven or more means you have a solid base. Fewer than five usually means you are losing enquiries today.

Takeaway: A good website is not about looking fancy. It is about being clear, fast and easy to contact.
