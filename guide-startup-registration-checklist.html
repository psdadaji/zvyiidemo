A starter checklist for registering a business in India

5 min read · By the Zvyii team

Before you start

Requirements depend on your business type, activity and state, and rules change from time to time. Use this as a general checklist and confirm current requirements for your case.

The usual steps

  1. Choose a structure. Common options are proprietorship, partnership, LLP, One Person Company and Private Limited Company. Each differs in liability, compliance and funding options.
  2. Get a PAN and open a current account in the name of the business.
  3. Register the entity as required for your structure.
  4. Check GST. Whether you need it depends on turnover, the kind of supply and how you sell, including online and inter-state sales.
  5. Consider Udyam (MSME) registration for small business benefits.
  6. Protect your brand with a trademark for your name and logo.
  7. Add sector licences if needed, such as FSSAI for food or an Import Export Code for trade.
  8. Plan your compliance calendar. Annual filings and returns have deadlines.

Where people go wrong

  • Picking a structure without thinking about future funding or partners.
  • Delaying trademark until after the brand is popular.
  • Missing annual filing dates.
Takeaway: Get the structure right first, then layer tax, brand protection and licences. Zvyii is building registration and compliance support to help with these steps.
