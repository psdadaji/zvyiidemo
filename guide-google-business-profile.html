Get found on Google Maps

4 min read · By the Zvyii team

Why it matters

When someone searches "near me", Google often shows a map with three businesses first. Your Google Business Profile decides whether you are one of them.

Set it up in seven steps

  1. Claim and verify your profile with your exact business name.
  2. Pick the right main category and add secondary ones.
  3. Add accurate details: address, phone, website and opening hours.
  4. List your services with short descriptions.
  5. Upload real photos of your place, team and work.
  6. Ask happy customers for reviews and reply to every review.
  7. Post updates such as offers and news regularly.

Keep it consistent

Use the same name, address and phone number on your website, social pages and directories. Consistency helps Google trust your information.

Takeaway: A complete, active profile with real reviews is one of the most affordable ways to get local customers.
