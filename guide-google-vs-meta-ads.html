Google Ads or Meta Ads: which one first?

5 min read · By the Zvyii team

The short answer

If people already search for what you sell, start with Google Ads. If your product needs to be seen to be wanted, start with Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram). Most businesses eventually use both.

Google Ads: catching people who are ready

Google shows your ad when someone types a search, like "dentist near me" or "office furniture supplier". The person already has a need, so leads tend to be warmer. The trade-off is that popular keywords can be expensive.

Meta Ads: creating interest

Meta shows your ad to people based on interests, location and behaviour while they scroll. It works well for visual products, offers, events and local awareness, and usually reaches more people for the same budget. Leads can need more follow-up.

How to choose

  • Clear search demand and a service people look for: Google Ads first.
  • Visual product, fashion, food, real estate or a new offer: Meta Ads first.
  • B2B enquiries: Google Ads and a strong landing page, with Meta for retargeting.

Before you spend a rupee

  • Have one clear landing page with one action.
  • Set up conversion tracking for calls, forms and WhatsApp clicks.
  • Start with one platform and one offer, then compare.
Takeaway: Start where your customer already is. Search for intent, social for discovery. Track every enquiry so you know what works.
